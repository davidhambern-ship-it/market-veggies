Avocado: supplies dietary fiber and mainly unsaturated fats.

Garlic: crushing it helps create the compounds behind its familiar flavor and aroma.

Move it: physical activity uses energy from food; it does not switch nutrients on or off.

🧠 Carry's Food Science

Carrots: contain beta-carotene, which the body can convert to vitamin A to support normal vision.

Cooking: softens carrots and can make some beta-carotene more accessible during digestion.

Moving: the body uses energy during play; exercise doesn't activate food nutrients.

Kitchen safety: a grown-up handles real hot pots and sharp tools.

CARRY'S RECIPE

Crunchy Carrot Wraps

YOU'LL NEED

  • Whole-grain tortillas
  • Shredded carrots
  • Cucumber strips
  • Hummus or another favorite spread
  • Optional leafy greens

MAKE IT

  1. Spread hummus over the tortilla.
  2. Let kids arrange the vegetables.
  3. Roll, slice and crunch.
🏃 Orange Power RelaySet up a short relay using marching, hopping, side-steps and a silly finish-line dance.

TOMMY'S RECIPE

Tomato Pizza Pitas

YOU'LL NEED

  • Whole-grain pita rounds
  • Tomato sauce
  • Shredded cheese
  • Colorful chopped vegetables

MAKE IT

  1. Spread sauce over each pita.
  2. Let kids design a veggie face.
  3. Add cheese and bake with adult supervision until warm and melty.
⚡ Market Move CircuitDo 20 seconds each of jumping, reaching, balancing, marching and dancing. Repeat if everybody wants another round.

NANNER'S RECIPE

Banana Oat Bites

YOU'LL NEED

  • Ripe banana
  • Rolled oats
  • Cinnamon
  • Optional nut or seed butter if appropriate for your household

MAKE IT

  1. Mash the banana.
  2. Mix in oats and cinnamon.
  3. Shape into small bites and bake with adult supervision until set.
🧘 Banana Balance ChallengeInvent five safe balance poses. Hold each one, switch sides and let kids name their own poses.

GARY'S RECIPE

Garlic Garden Toast

YOU'LL NEED

  • Whole-grain bread
  • Olive oil
  • A small amount of minced garlic
  • Chopped tomato and herbs

MAKE IT

  1. Mix a small amount of garlic with olive oil.
  2. Brush lightly onto bread and toast with adult supervision.
  3. Top with tomato and herbs.
