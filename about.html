ABOUT THE SERIES

A farmers market where every aisle can become an adventure.

Market Veggies turns ordinary produce into a cast of characters kids can root for, laugh with and learn from.

THE WORLD

Fresh Produce. Big Adventures.

The market is more than a place to shop — it is a neighborhood full of personalities, friendships, challenges and surprises.

THE IDEA

Story first. Learning naturally.

Kids come for Gary, Carry, Artie, Tommy and the crew. Along the way, they pick up food knowledge, curiosity and healthy-life skills.
