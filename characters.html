MEET THE MARKET CREW

Good food has some pretty great personalities.

Each Market Veggie has a story to tell — including what makes their fruit or vegetable a smart addition to a varied, balanced diet.

🧄 GARLIC

Gary the Garlic

“I may be little, but I bring BIG flavor!”

Garlic belongs to the allium family. It adds strong flavor to food, which can make vegetables, grains and proteins more exciting without needing lots of extra salt or heavy sauces.

  • Contains naturally occurring sulfur compounds.
  • Adds flavor to soups, sauces, vegetables and more.
  • A little can go a long way.
🥕 CARROT

Carry the Carrot

“Crunch first. Courage second!”

Carrots are naturally rich in beta-carotene, which the body can turn into vitamin A. They also provide fiber and make an easy crunchy snack.

  • Beta-carotene helps support normal vision and immune function.
  • Provides dietary fiber.
  • Great raw, roasted, shredded or blended.
🌿 ARTICHOKE

Artie the Artichoke

“Sometimes the best stuff is worth peeling for.”

Artichokes are packed with edible leaves and a tender heart. They provide fiber and folate and are a fun food for kids to explore with their hands.

  • A good source of fiber.
  • Provides folate.
  • Trying unfamiliar foods can be part of the adventure.
🍅 TOMATO

Tommy the Tomato

“I bring the color — and the sauce!”

Tomatoes provide vitamin C, potassium and lycopene, a plant pigment responsible for much of their red color. They show up in everything from salads to sauces.

  • Provides vitamin C and potassium.
  • Contains lycopene.
  • Works raw, roasted, simmered or blended.
🍌
🍌 BANANA

Nanner the Banana

“Peel, smile, move!”

Bananas provide carbohydrates, potassium and vitamin B6. They are soft, portable and easy to add to breakfast, snacks or smoothies.

  • Provides potassium.
  • Contains vitamin B6.
  • Carbohydrates are one source of energy for active bodies.
🫚
🫚 GINGER

Gingy the Ginger

“A little zing changes everything.”

Ginger is a fragrant root used in cuisines around the world. It brings warm, spicy flavor to foods and drinks and helps kids discover new tastes.

  • Can be used fresh, dried or ground.
  • Pairs with fruits, vegetables, grains and savory dishes.
  • A great example of how herbs and spices can make healthy foods exciting.
