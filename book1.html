MEET OUR UNLIKELY HERO

Gary doesn't quite fit in. That doesn't stop him from showing up.

Gary the Garlic just wants to play, laugh, and feel welcome among the fruits and vegetables at the farmers market. But the other produce keeps its distance because of his strong smell.

Then a mysterious sickness sweeps through the market. With his neighbors in trouble, Gary faces a choice: stay on the sidelines, or use his unique strengths to help.

It's an imaginative adventure about finding courage, choosing kindness, and realizing that everyone has something valuable to offer.
